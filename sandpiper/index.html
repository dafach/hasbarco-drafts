Do you qualify?

Sandpiper offers income-restricted homes — rent is set below market for households within certain income limits. The apartments open right now are subsidized (Project-Based Voucher) homes for extremely-low-income households — rent is about 30% of your income, and the subsidy is attached to the apartment, so you don’t need your own voucher to apply.

Frequently asked questions

Who can live at Sandpiper?
Households earning within the income limits for each apartment. Studios and lofts fit up to 2 people; one-bedrooms up to 3. The apartments open right now are for extremely-low-income households — up to about $38,215/year for 1 person, $43,710 for 2, or $49,170 for 3.
How much is rent?
The homes available right now are subsidized: you pay about 30% of your household income and a Project-Based Voucher covers the rest — no voucher of your own needed. To qualify, a household can earn up to about $38,215 (1 person), $43,710 (2), or $49,170 (3) per year. (Non-subsidized tax-credit homes rent from about $1,593 to $2,049/month, depending on the apartment and your income, for households earning up to roughly $76,500–$98,340.)
Do you accept Section 8 vouchers?
Yes — Housing Choice Vouchers are welcome and can lower your share of the rent. Ask us and we’ll explain how it works.
What do I need to apply?
Bring a photo ID, proof of all household income (recent pay stubs, or an employer letter, plus any benefit award letters), and your most recent bank/asset statements. Ask us and we’ll walk you through the full checklist.
Is it safe and well-managed?
Sandpiper is professionally managed by the Housing Authority of the County of Santa Barbara, with on-site management and resident support services. The community has been newly renovated and is under new management.
