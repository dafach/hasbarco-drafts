Do you qualify?

Cypress Court is income-restricted senior housing — you must be 55 or older and earn within the income limits. The limit depends on how many people are in your household. Many homes are for lower-income seniors, so ask us and we’ll check for you.

Maximum household income by household size
People in your homeMost you can earn / year
1 personup to $76,500
2 peopleup to $87,420
3 peopleup to $98,340

These are the highest incomes that can qualify. Many apartments are for seniors earning far less — from about $38,250 (1 person) or $43,710 (2 people) at the deepest-affordable level. Not sure? Send us the form below and we’ll check for you.

Frequently asked questions

Who can live at Cypress Court?
Adults 55 and older whose household earns within the income limits. One-bedrooms fit up to 2 people; two-bedrooms up to 3. Ask us and we’ll check your household in a few minutes.
How much is rent?
Rent is based on your income. One-bedrooms run about $993–$1,986/month and two-bedrooms $1,191–$2,383, depending on your income level — with many homes set at the lower, deeper-affordable end for seniors on a fixed income.
Do you accept Section 8 vouchers?
Yes — Housing Choice Vouchers are welcome and can lower your share of the rent. Ask us and we’ll explain how it works.
What do I need to apply?
Bring your ID documents and income verification. Ask us and we’ll walk you through exactly what to bring.
Is it safe and well-managed?
Cypress Court is professionally managed by the Housing Authority of the County of Santa Barbara, with on-site management, a community room, garden, computer lab, and media lounge.
